Ritual

Two hostels in two cities, one sharing ritual. Every set-up in her world has a twin in his: the same shape, height and place in frame, dressed in a different climate. The match is what lets a tablet screen feel like a shared sofa, bench, window and bed.

WORLD BIBLE

Different worlds, matched shapes

Her snow is written into the script (it falls outside her window), so her hostel sits in a cold hill town and his in a warm city. Silk purple is the only saturated colour in both worlds.

PRODUCTION

Notes for the shoot

One camera recipe

Every matched pair uses the same lens, camera height and tilt in both cities (35mm, about 1 m for seated set-ups). Shoot his plates first, then use a printed line overlay of his frame on her monitor.

Screens in post

All tablet and phone screens are composited. Shoot clean plates with tracking markers and keep the screens dark on set to avoid spill on faces.

The foam gag

Foam must read as snow on her screen, so backlight his window to make the foam glow white. Test on a spare pane first; the can is a hero prop with the label turned away.
